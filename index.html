Roshan Kenia

I'm a PhD student in Biomedical Informatics at Harvard Medical School, in the AI in Medicine track, where I'm advised by William Lotter. My research focuses on biomedical image analysis, deep learning, weakly supervised learning, and interpretable 3D attention. I completed my MS in Computer Science at Columbia University, where I was a member of the AI4VS Lab, and my BS in Computer Science and Applied Math & Statistics at Stony Brook University, advised by Zhaozheng Yin. I also did a co-op with MIT Lincoln Laboratory in the Human Health and Performance Systems group, advised by Lars Gjesteby. I'm an NSF Graduate Research Fellow (GRFP) and a Breyer Fellow.

Research

I'm interested in biomedical image analysis, deep learning, weakly supervised learning, and interpretable 3D attention.

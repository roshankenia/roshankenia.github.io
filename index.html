Roshan Kenia

I'm a PhD student in Biomedical Informatics at Harvard Medical School, in the AI in Medicine track, where I'm advised by William Lotter. My research spans anatomically-informed deep learning, foundation model adaptation, and the evaluation of medical AI systems in clinical settings. I completed my MS in Computer Science at Columbia University, where I was a member of the AI4VS Lab, and my BS in Computer Science and Applied Math & Statistics at Stony Brook University, advised by Zhaozheng Yin. I also did a co-op with MIT Lincoln Laboratory in the Human Health and Performance Systems group, advised by Lars Gjesteby. I'm an NSF Graduate Research Fellow (GRFP) and a Breyer Fellow.

Research

Most of my work is about building AI methods for medical imaging that are both accurate and interpretable, often by incorporating anatomical structure or clinician-guided supervision.
